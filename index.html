How a project runs

Five steps, in this order, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Walkthrough

    We visit the site, listen to your goals and look at what's already there.

  2. Estimate

    You get a written estimate broken down by trade, with no vague lump sums.

  3. Plans and permits

    We finalize drawings, order long-lead materials and handle the permit paperwork.

  4. Build

    One site lead runs the crew, keeps the schedule and updates you every week.

  5. Handover

    We walk the finished work with you, fix the punch list and hand over the keys.
